## 🚦 The 3-Tier Daily Buying Clearance Protocol

Column 6 MUST assign one of three standardized verdicts for each trading day:

### 🟢 1. CLEARED FOR BUYING (Green Light — High Conviction Accumulation)
* **Conditions:**
  1. Macro tide is positive or neutral (SPY/QQQ holding above 20-EMA; TLT stable).
  2. No high-impact binary events scheduled within 4 hours.
  3. Market breadth is positive (>60% advancing volume).
* **Execution Directive:**
  * Clearance granted to deploy capital into 4/4 Gate Fortress Compounders (`AMKR`, `NVDA`, `SOFI`).
  * Limit orders placed strictly at **S1 Support Floors** during the **Golden Morning Dip Window (9:45 AM – 10:30 AM EDT)**.

### 🟡 2. CAUTIOUS / S2-S3 FLOORS ONLY (Yellow Light — Staged Limit Bids)
* **Conditions:**
  1. Pre-catalyst anticipation (e.g. CPI morning, pre-FOMC 2:00 PM release).
  2. Mild downward pressure (2–3 macro signals flashing yellow).
* **Execution Directive:**
  * **Strict Ban on S1 entries** (S1 is likely to be swept by algorithmic stop hunts).
  * Staged Limit Orders placed strictly at deep **S2 or S3 Capitulation Floors**.
  * Sizing restricted to **Tranche 1 only (30% to 33% of target allocation)**.

### 🔴 3. STRICT BUYING BAN / $0.00 ALLOCATION (Red Light — 100% Capital Freeze)
* **Mandatory Trigger Conditions:**
  1. **Thursday & Friday Negative Market Signals:** If index breadth, semiconductor indices (`SOXX`), bond yields, or geopolitical events turn negative on Thursday or Friday, **ACTIVATE AN IMMEDIATE 100% BUYING BAN**.
  2. **High Downward Pressure Regime (4/5 Signals Red):** Surging crude oil (Brent >$95–$100), bond selloff (TLT dumping), or index futures down >-0.5%.
  3. **T-Minus 48-Hour Earnings Ban:** Strict ban on buying any stock within 48 hours of its earnings report (`post_earnings_dip_discipline_rule.md`).
  4. **Pre-Event 2:00 PM Freeze:** Strict ban on buying before high-impact 2:00 PM Fed rate announcements or meeting minutes.
  5. **Depleted War-Chest ($1,500 Buffer):** When liquid buying power falls below 10%–15% dry powder mandate.
* **Execution Directive:**

## 📅 The Monday-to-Friday Weekly Entry Timing Framework

Every weekly audit MUST evaluate trading days through this institutional framework:

1. **Monday (Tide Verification & S1 Accumulation):**
   * Review weekend geopolitical news and foreign market gaps.
   * If futures gaps are absorbed and market breadth holds green past 9:45 AM EDT, clear for selective S1 limit buys.
2. **Tuesday (Prime Strategic Accumulation Window):**
   * Statistically the lowest-volatility accumulation day of the week.
   * Optimal day to build positions in earnings-catalyst winners ahead of mid-week reports.
3. **Wednesday (Mid-Week Binary Event Gate):**
   * **Banned Period (9:30 AM – 2:00 PM EDT):** Pre-FOMC / Pre-CPI bid drought. Algorithms pull liquidity; retail gets whipsawed.
   * **Post-Catalyst Window (2:15 PM – 2:45 PM EDT):** If the Fed announcement or CPI prints favorably and S1/S2 floors hold, clearance is granted for staged buys.
4. **Thursday (Macro Momentum or De-Risking Gate):**
   * If jobless claims or geopolitical events trigger downward pressure, **DO NOT BUY**.
   * If market breadth is positive and sector rotation favors our holdings, stage selective limit entries at verified floors.
5. **Friday (Weekend De-Grossing & Options Expiration — Default Defensive Day):**
   * **STRICT BAN IF THURSDAY OR FRIDAY SIGNALS ARE NEGATIVE.**
   * Institutional hedge funds systematically reduce gross exposure and sell high-beta tech into Friday closes to avoid weekend headline risk.
   * New entries on Friday are prohibited unless all 5 Macro Signals are 100% green and VIX is collapsing below 14.
