# Rule: Priority FCF & New Cash in Bank Solvency Runway Rule

This priority rule governs all stock selection, trade execution, and portfolio management.

## Core Directives & Formulas

### 1. Free Cash Flow (FCF) Formula
- **Formula:** `FCF = Cash Flow from Operations (CFO) - Capital Expenditures (CapEx)`
- Verify true cash generated by operations after all CapEx infrastructure investments.

### 2. New Cash in Bank Trajectory Formula
- **Formula:** `New Cash in Bank = Old Cash in Bank + Free Cash Flow (FCF)`
- Every recommended or audited stock MUST demonstrate a positive `New Cash in Bank` trajectory.

### 3. Net Cash Surplus After Debt Formula (MANDATORY IN ALL TABLES)
- **Formula:** `Net Cash Surplus After Debt = New Cash in Bank - Total Corporate Debt`
- Measures the TRUE net liquid cash remaining after paying off 100% of all corporate debt liabilities.

### 4. Solvency & Safety Zone Classification
- 🟢 **SAFE ZONE (4+ Years Runway & Solvency Safety):** Positive Net Cash Surplus After Debt (or 4+ Yrs FCF Runway). Company is 100% safe, solvent, and protected from debt default.
- 🔴 **HIGH RISK DANGEROUS ZONE (Bankruptcy & Emergency Dilution Danger):** Cash in Bank close to Zero + Negative Net Surplus + Negative FCF. **IMMEDIATE DISQUALIFICATION / DO NOT BUY!**

### 5. Mandatory Table Integration
- Every stock audit table MUST explicitly include:
  1. `Free Cash Flow (FCF = CFO - CapEx)`
  2. `New Cash in Bank = Old Cash + FCF`
  3. `Total Debt & Interest Rate Expense`
  4. `Net Cash Surplus After Debt = New Cash - Debt`
  5. `Solvency Safety Zone & Runway (Safe Zone 4+ Yrs vs High Risk)`
