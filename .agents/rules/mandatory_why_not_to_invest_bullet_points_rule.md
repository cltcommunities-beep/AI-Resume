# 🔴 RED-TAG MANDATORY RULE: UPFRONT "WHY NOT TO INVEST" FORENSIC RED FLAGS STANDARD

## 🚨 MANDATORY EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE
The assistant is **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** from presenting any stock analysis, price target, or buy recommendation without FIRST including a prominent, unskippable section titled:

### `🛑 Mandatory Forensic Red Flags: Why You Should NOT Invest`

Retail investors routinely lose capital because brokers and analysts highlight bullish revenue growth, exciting AI headlines, and discounted price drops, while hiding the catastrophic risks. This leads the user to assume a stock is at the "best entry position" when it is actually an active trap (such as CoreWeave's $35.6B debt and 35M share ATM dilution).

From this day forward, **the bear case and forensic dealbreakers MUST be stated upfront in clear bullet points before any capital is committed.** **NO EXCEPTIONS PERMITTED.**

---

## 🏛️ 1. The 5 Mandatory "Why NOT to Invest" Bullet Points

For EVERY stock reviewed or considered for purchase, the assistant MUST provide concrete, data-backed bullet points covering the following 5 dimensions:

1. **🚩 Active Dilution & Share-Printing Risk:**
   * Does the company have an active **At-The-Market (ATM) equity program**?
   * Has management filed an **S-3 shelf registration** allowing them or insiders to dump millions of shares on retail buyers?
   * If yes: State the exact share count authorized to dilute the float.
2. **🚩 Toxic Debt & Interest Payment Burden:**
   * Are corporate bond yields trading in distressed territory (**>10% to 12%+**)?
   * How much cash does the company bleed every quarter *just to pay bank interest*?
   * Does interest expense eat **>15% of total revenue**?
3. **🚩 Customer Concentration & Moat Vulnerability:**
   * Is more than 50% of revenue dependent on just 1 to 3 anchor customers (e.g. CoreWeave relying 72% on Microsoft, OpenAI, and Meta)?
   * Are those customers actively building their own custom chips or in-house data centers to eliminate their supplier?
4. **🚩 Free Cash Flow Burn & The CapEx Treadmill:**
   * Is Free Cash Flow (FCF) negative?
   * Does the company have to constantly burn billions of dollars buying rapidly depreciating hardware just to stay relevant?
5. **🚩 Tactical Timing & Technical Trap:**
   * Is the current price sitting right below overhead **R1 / R2 Resistance**?
   * Is the stock trading outside the **Golden Morning Dip Window (9:45 AM – 10:30 AM EDT)**?
   * Is there an impending quarterly earnings report within 2 weeks that will trigger institutional de-grossing?
