# Rule: Institutional 12-Point Fundamental & Technical Due Diligence Rule

This rule governs all stock analyses, valuation audits, trade setups, and portfolio management executed for the user. Every stock evaluation MUST be audited against the following 12 institutional standards:

## The 12 Institutional Standards

### 1. Mandatory Executive Opening Bell Portfolio Readiness Table (PRIORITY MANDATORY)
- **MANDATORY:** For EVERY stock analyzed, the response MUST include the **Executive Opening Bell Portfolio Readiness Table** featuring ALL 17 mandatory columns (🏷️ 🚀 🛡️ 🏦 💳 🏗️ 📜 🧮 🏆 <ctrl42> 🟢 🔴 📜 📈 🛡️ 🎯 📊) including Column 15: 🛡️ **Risk Management Principles Compliance (Score, Position Size & Recommended Buy Directives)**!

### 2. S1, S2, and S3 Technical Candle Support Floor Audit
- Every stock MUST explicitly detail 3 distinct technical candle support levels in Column 3 (S1 50 SMA, S2 100 SMA, S3 200 SMA).

### 3. 7 Golden Institutional Risk Management Principles & Stop Loss Buffer
- Recommended Buy is placed AT the S1 support floor (e.g. `$53.33`).
- Stop Loss is placed **-2% to -3% BELOW S1** (e.g. `$51.80`) to provide breathing room and avoid getting stopped out by false intra-day 1-cent spikes!

### 4. Mandatory Dual Future Surplus Tracking
- **Formula 1 (Prepayments):** `(New Cash in Bank + Upfront Prepayments) - Total Corporate Debt`
- **Formula 2 (3-Year CFO):** `(New Cash in Bank + 3-Year Operating Cash Flow) - Total Corporate Debt`

### 5. Next Quarter Fundamentals Outlook Audit (Column 14)
- Audit consensus next quarter revenue growth, net margin trajectory, and earnings momentum to classify as **🟢 POSITIVE TO INVEST**, **🟡 NEUTRAL / HOLD**, or **🔴 SCARY SELL-OFF RISK (Stock Will Drop!)**.

### 6. Unissued Authorized Shares Audit
- Audit total SEC authorized shares vs issued shares outstanding to measure the exact **Unissued Authorized Shares Remaining** for future dilution capacity warning.

### 7. Pre-Paid Contracts & Upfront Cash Inflows Verification
- Audit whether companies hold **Upfront Customer Pre-Paid Contracts vs Total Contract Backlog Values** (e.g. `NBIS` $9.0B pre-paid / $19.4B total backlog, `GOOG` $18.5B pre-paid / $106B total backlog).

### 8. Priority FCF & New Cash in Bank Solvency Runway Rule
- **FCF Formula:** `FCF = CFO - CapEx`
- **New Cash in Bank Formula:** `New Cash in Bank = Old Cash in Bank + FCF`
- **Safety Zone:** 🟢 **SAFE ZONE (4+ Years Runway)** vs 🔴 **HIGH RISK (Near $0 Cash + Neg FCF)**.

### 9. Mandatory Visual Emoji Icons for Every Table Column
- **MANDATORY:** Every table column header MUST feature a distinct, vibrant visual emoji icon to highlight key metrics instantly.

### 10. Free Cash Flow (FCF) Verification
- Verify true Free Cash Flow (FCF = Operating Cash Flow - CapEx). Do not rely solely on paper GAAP net income.

### 11. Share Dilution & Buyback Status (MANDATORY IN ALL TABLES)
- Audit stock-based compensation (SBC), convertible debt conversion prices, warrant exercises, secondary offerings, insider lock-up expiration dates, and active share buyback programs.
- **MANDATORY:** Every stock table MUST explicitly include a dedicated 🔴 **Share Dilution & Buyback Status** field and 📜 **Unissued Authorized Shares** field!

### 12. Strict No-LaTeX Formatting Rule
- **NEVER** use LaTeX math blocks (`$$...$$`) or LaTeX code tags. Deliver all formulas in plain text inside markdown tables.
