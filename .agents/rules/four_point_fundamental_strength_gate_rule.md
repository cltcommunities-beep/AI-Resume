# MANDATORY 4-POINT FUNDAMENTAL STRENGTH GATE RULE

## Executive Directive
Every single stock analysis, audit, or evaluation MUST include a **separate 4-Point Fundamental Strength Gate Table** displayed BEFORE the 17-Column Solvency Audit Table. This gate determines whether a stock is fundamentally investable or speculative.

A stock MUST PASS ALL 4 GATES to qualify as a "Fundamentally Strong" investment. If ANY gate fails, the stock MUST be flagged as "SPECULATIVE / HIGH RISK" in all reports.

---

## The 4 Mandatory Gates

### Gate 1: 🏦 Net Cash Surplus (Cash > Debt)
- **PASS:** Total Cash & Equivalents > Total Debt = Positive Net Cash Surplus
- **FAIL:** Total Debt > Total Cash = Negative Net Cash (Debt Trap)
- **Data Source:** Latest 10-Q or 10-K Balance Sheet

### Gate 2: 💰 Profitable (Positive Net Income)
- **PASS:** Positive Net Income for at least 2 consecutive quarters
- **FAIL:** Net Loss in any of the last 2 quarters = Cash-Burning / Pre-Profit Company
- **Data Source:** Latest 2 quarterly earnings reports (10-Q)
- **CRITICAL:** Government grants, one-time gains, and unrealized investment gains do NOT count as real profit

### Gate 3: 🧮 Positive Free Cash Flow (CFO > CapEx)
- **PASS:** Cash Flow from Operations (CFO) minus Capital Expenditures (CapEx) = Positive Free Cash Flow
- **FAIL:** Negative FCF = Company cannot fund itself from operations
- **Data Source:** Latest 10-Q Cash Flow Statement

### Gate 4: 📜 Real Prepaid Customer Revenue (Not Grants or Future Promises)
- **PASS:** Company receives upfront cash payments from real customers (e.g., customer deposits, subscription revenue, prepaid contracts, product sales)
- **FAIL:** Revenue consists primarily of government grants, reimbursements, or future contract promises that generate $0 cash today
- **Data Source:** Revenue breakdown in latest 10-Q, contract disclosures, and customer prepayment notes

---

## Mandatory Table Format

The 4-Point Fundamental Strength Gate Table MUST be rendered in the following format for EVERY stock analyzed:

| Gate # | Fundamental Check | Status (PASS / FAIL) | Evidence & Numbers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🏦 Gate 1 | Net Cash Surplus (Cash > Debt) | ✅ PASS or ❌ FAIL | $X.XB Cash - $X.XB Debt = +/-$X.XB |
| 💰 Gate 2 | Profitable (2 Consecutive Quarters) | ✅ PASS or ❌ FAIL | Q1: +/-$XM / Q2: +/-$XM Net Income |
| 🧮 Gate 3 | Positive Free Cash Flow (CFO > CapEx) | ✅ PASS or ❌ FAIL | $XM CFO - $XM CapEx = +/-$XM FCF |
| 📜 Gate 4 | Real Prepaid Customer Revenue | ✅ PASS or ❌ FAIL | $XB in real customer cash / $0 in grant-only revenue |

**Final Verdict Row:**
- ✅✅✅✅ **4/4 PASSED = "FUNDAMENTALLY STRONG — INVESTABLE"**
- ✅✅✅❌ **3/4 PASSED = "MODERATE — INVESTABLE WITH CAUTION"**
- ✅✅❌❌ **2/4 PASSED = "WEAK FUNDAMENTALS — SPECULATIVE"**
- ✅❌❌❌ or ❌❌❌❌ **1/4 or 0/4 = "DO NOT INVEST — HIGH RISK SPECULATIVE"**

---

## Integration with 17-Column Solvency Table

1. The 4-Point Gate Table MUST appear BEFORE the 17-Column Solvency Audit Table.
2. The Gate verdict (e.g., "4/4 PASSED" or "2/4 FAILED") MUST be referenced inside Column 14 (Next Quarter Fundamentals Outlook) of the 17-Column Table.
3. If a stock fails Gate 2 (Not Profitable) AND Gate 3 (Negative FCF), it MUST carry a 🔴 "SPECULATIVE / HIGH RISK" tag in Column 15 (Risk Management Compliance).
