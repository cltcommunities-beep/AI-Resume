**Every proposed entry must pass BOTH the Quality Gate AND the Entry-Price Valuation Gate.**

## 🛡️ 2. The 5 Mandatory Pre-Entry Valuation Gates

Before committing $1.00 to ANY stock (even Bucket A monopolies), the assistant and trader must verify:

### Gate 1: Ban on Mid-Air Buying
* **The Rule:** Purchases are strictly forbidden when the stock is trading extended in mid-air (>3% above its 20-Day Moving Average Floor or >5% above its 50MFA).
* **Execution:** Orders must be placed **exclusively as GTC Limit Buys at defined S1, S2, or S3 Support Floors**.

### Gate 2: The S2/S3 Support-Floor Staging Standard
* **S1 (Immediate Base):** 20-Day Moving Average Floor (e.g. `NVDA` @ $216.00).
* **S2 (Secondary Pullback Floor):** 50-Day Moving Average / Prior Breakout Retest.
* **S3 (Capitulation Value Base):** Major multi-week double-bottom or 0.618/0.65 Fibonacci Golden Pocket (e.g. `SPCX` @ $143.50, `NBIS` @ $160.00).

### Gate 3: The Anti-FOMO 3-Tranche Scaling Law
* **The Rule:** Never enter 100% of an intended position in a single order.
* **Allocation:**
  * **Tranche 1 (33%):** Initial test at S1 support floor.
  * **Tranche 2 (33%):** Secondary add at S2 support or upon confirmed daily reversal candle.
  * **Tranche 3 (34%):** Dry-powder reserve for macro washouts or S3 capitulation.

### Gate 4: Active Capital Recycling Mandate
* If an anchor holding surges into short-term resistance (e.g. `SPCX` to $166.00 on headlines) while the broader macroeconomic gauges (10Y Yield >5.0%, Crude >$100, QQQ red) are rolling over:
  * **Do not passively hold through the drop.**
  * **Sell 25%–50% into the spike** to lock in green cash.
  * **Immediately queue a lower GTC Limit Buy at S2/S3 support** (e.g. @ $150.00–$152.00) to re-accumulate shares cheaper and permanently reduce average cost.

### Gate 5: Valuation Guardrail (Forward PEG Ceiling)
* Forward Price/Earnings-to-Growth (PEG) must not exceed **1.5x**.
* Free Cash Flow (FCF) Yield must be positive and sustainable; never pay >50x Sales for non-cash-flow narratives.
