## 🏛️ 2. The 4 Mandatory Dependency & Competition Laws

### Law 1: ⬆️ Upstream Supplier Shock & Basket Contagion Rule (`NVDA`/`TSM` -> `NBIS`/`CRWV`/`AMKR`)
* When a primary upstream supplier (`NVDA` or `TSM`) suffers a **≥3.0% drop** due to regulatory probes, export restrictions, or chip delays, Wall Street ETF and quant algorithms automatically trigger **Basket Selling** across all dependent midstream stocks (`NBIS`, `CRWV`, `AMKR`, `SMCI`) at the 9:30 AM opening bell.
* **Strict Rule:** Even if a downstream partner (`NBIS`) is flat in thin pre-market trading, **NEVER place an aggressive pre-market or opening limit buy order** while its core supplier (`NVDA`) is down ≥3.0%. Wait for regular-session volume to absorb the basket selling.

### Law 2: ⬇️ Downstream Customer CapEx & Oversight Rule (`MSFT`/`META` -> `NBIS`/`CRWV`/`NVDA`)
* Over 60% of AI infrastructure revenue flows from 4 hyperscale customers (`MSFT`, `META`, `GOOG`, `AMZN`).
* Whenever a major customer announces tightened project oversight, delayed data center sign-offs, or CapEx digestion, the assistant must immediately audit whether the target stock's revenue is protected by **upfront pre-paid, binding take-or-pay contracts** (`NBIS` standard) vs. **high-interest debt-funded speculative capacity** (`CRWV` trap).

### Law 3: ⚔️ 72-Hour Direct Competitor Earnings Contagion Rule (`CRWV` <-> `NBIS`)
* Direct competitors trade as a **linked sympathy pair** during earnings season.
* If a direct Tier-1 competitor (`CRWV`) reports quarterly earnings within **72 hours** (e.g., `CRWV` reporting Tuesday PM ahead of `NBIS` reporting Thursday AM), any miss, CapEx blowout, or weak guidance from the competitor will immediately drag the peer stock down -5% to -15% in sympathy.
* **Strict Rule:** Enforce an automatic **🔴 100% Capital Allocation Freeze ($0.00)** on any stock whose direct competitor reports earnings within **72 hours**, or which reports its own earnings within **72 hours**.

### Law 4: 🎯 Fundamental Separation vs. Sympathy Drag Arbitrage
* The assistant must always separate **True Supply-Chain Contagion** (a problem that hurts both companies' earnings) from **False Algorithmic Sympathy** (a headline specific to one company that actually benefits the other).
* *Example:* If `NVDA` faces a Congressional probe over chip smuggling into China (`Megaspeed`), that does **not** impair `NBIS`'s 100% Western-compliant U.S. and European data centers—in fact, stricter China export enforcement redirects more Nvidia GPU supply to compliant Western partners like `NBIS`. Once the 72-hour earnings/probe window clears, accumulate the fundamentally insulated winner on the sympathy dip.
