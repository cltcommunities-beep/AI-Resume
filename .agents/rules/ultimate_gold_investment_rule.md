# THE ULTIMATE GOLD INVESTMENT RULE

## Executive Directive
The assistant MUST enforce the **Ultimate Gold Investment Rule** for all stock recommendations, portfolio audits, and capital allocations. A stock is STRICTLY PROHIBITED from being recommended or purchased unless it satisfies ALL FOUR of the following mandatory conditions:

---

## The 4 Mandatory Gold Criteria

### 1. 💰 Positive Net Income & Earnings Beat
- **Requirement:** Company MUST be profitable with positive net income for at least 2 consecutive quarters.
- **Prohibition:** Pre-profit, cash-burning, or loss-making companies are STRICTLY BANNED.

### 2. 📈 Strong Business Growth & Real Customer Revenue
- **Requirement:** Company MUST demonstrate high YoY revenue/business growth from real customer sales, subscriptions, deposits, or commercial product orders (e.g., Apple, GM, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Google).
- **Prohibition:** Revenue from government grants, reimbursements, or future contract promises paying $0 today is STRICTLY BANNED.

### 3. 🏆 4-Point Fundamental Strength Gate (4/4 Score)
- **Requirement:** Company MUST pass the 4-Point Fundamental Gate (Cash > Debt, Profitable, +FCF, Real Customer Revenue).

### 4. 🧮 100% Debt Clearance by Formula 1 or Formula 2 PLUS Positive Cash Flow After Cleaning
- **Requirement:** Total Debt MUST be 100% CLEARED by Formula 1 (Prepaid Cash) or Formula 2 (3-Year Cumulative Operating Cash Flow), AND the company MUST maintain **Positive Free Cash Flow AFTER debt clearance** (+$X.XX Billion Net Cash Surplus).
- **Prohibition:** If F1 or F2 leaves a negative net debt balance remaining (e.g., Energy Transfer `ET`), the stock is STRICTLY BANNED from new capital allocation.
