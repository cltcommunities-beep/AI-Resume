## 🔬 4. Fundamental Invalidation Triggers (When to Actually Exit)
For stocks passing the 4-Point Fundamental Gate, a position is liquidated **ONLY IF** one or more of the following fundamental events occurs:
1. **Customer Contract Dissolution:** Loss of primary anchor customer/partner (e.g., TSMC or Apple terminating relationship).
2. **Profitability Collapse:** GAAP Net Income swings negative for two consecutive quarters without a capital reinvestment explanation.
3. **Insolvency Breach (Gate 1 Failure):** Debt liabilities surge past liquid cash reserves, reducing runway below 3 years.
4. **SEC Accounting Fraud / Material Misstatement:** Irregularities, CFO resignation under investigation, or delayed 10-Q filing.
