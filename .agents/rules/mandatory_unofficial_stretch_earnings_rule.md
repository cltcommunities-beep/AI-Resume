# 🔴 RED-TAG MANDATORY RULE: UNOFFICIAL STRETCH & BUY-SIDE WHISPER EARNINGS BENCHMARK

## 🚨 MANDATORY EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE
Whenever the user asks about **earnings, financial results, quarterly reports, revenue/EPS beats or misses, or guidance for ANY stock**, the assistant is **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** from simply reporting whether the company beat the official Wall Street sell-side consensus.

The assistant **MUST UNCONDITIONALLY DETERMINE AND CLEARLY STATE** whether the company **beat or missed the 🔬 Unofficial Stretch Result (The Buy-Side Whisper Target)** held privately by hedge funds and institutional trading desks. **NO EXCEPTIONS PERMITTED.**

---

## 🏛️ 1. Why This Rule Exists: The Retail Earnings Trap

Retail investors frequently get trapped buying post-earnings "beats" because public financial media (Yahoo Finance, CNBC, Robinhood) reports that a company beat the public sell-side consensus (e.g. Netflix beating $0.82 EPS by printing $0.87).

However, hedge funds and algorithmic market makers trade based on **Buy-Side Whisper Expectations (The 🔬 Unofficial Stretch Guess)**:
1. If a company beats the public estimate but **misses the 🔬 Unofficial Stretch Target**, institutions immediately dump the stock.
2. If a company beats both the public estimate **AND the 🔬 Unofficial Stretch Target**, it receives the **🏆 BEAT ALL 5 (TROPHY)**, fueling a multi-week institutional melt-up.

## 🏷️ 4. Standardized Status Coding & Nitpick Gap Analysis

1. 🏆 **`BEAT ALL 5 (TROPHY)`:**
   - **Condition:** The reported result beat Official Guidance, Wall Street Consensus, Tier-1 Banks, AND the **🔬 Unofficial Stretch Guess**.
   - **Meaning:** Authentic blow-out quarter; institutional green light.
2. 🔴 **`MISSED STRETCH (RED DOT)`:**
   - **Condition:** The reported result met or beat public consensus, but **failed to clear the 🔬 Unofficial Stretch Target** or missed future guidance.
   - **Meaning:** Hidden weakness; explains post-earnings sell-offs and prevents retail dip-buying traps.
3. 🔍 **`Nitpick Gap Analysis`:**
   - Explicitly highlight the exact dollar or percentage gap between the **🔬 Unofficial Stretch Target** and the actual reported number to decode institutional price action.

## 🚫 5. Strict Prohibitions
1. **NO Single-Number Claims:** Never answer "Did they beat earnings?" with a simple yes/no without breaking down past actuals vs future guidance vs the Unofficial Stretch Target.
2. **NO LaTeX Math Blocks:** Plain text numbers inside tables and markdown only (`no_latex_math_blocks_rule.md`).
3. **NO Blind Dip Buying:** Never recommend buying a post-earnings dip on Day 1 before the Day 2 Morning Dip Window (9:45 AM – 10:30 AM EDT) confirms support.
