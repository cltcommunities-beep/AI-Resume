# Rule: Dual Future Net Surplus Formula Architecture Rule

This priority rule permanently governs all stock Due Diligence, Solvency Audits, and Executive Table outputs for the user.

## Core Directives & Formulas

### 1. Mandatory Dual Future Surplus Tracking
Every stock audit MUST maintain and evaluate both Formula 1 and Formula 2 to measure true short-term and long-term liquidity:

### 2. Formula 1: Upfront Prepayments Future Net Surplus
- **Formula:** `Formula 1 Net Surplus = (New Cash in Bank + Upfront Cash Prepayments) - Total Corporate Debt`
- Measures immediate liquidity surplus added by customer cash prepayments before service delivery.

### 3. Formula 2: 3-Year Operating Cash Flow Future Net Surplus
- **Formula:** `Formula 2 Net Surplus = (New Cash in Bank + 3-Year Operating Cash Flow) - Total Corporate Debt`
- Measures long-term compounding liquidity surplus generated by core operating cash flow (CFO) over 3 years.

### 4. Mandatory Executive Table Integration
- Column #10 in the **Executive Opening Bell Portfolio Readiness Table** MUST display both Formula 1 and Formula 2 metrics side-by-side.
