## 🏛️ The 5-Engine Quantitative Growth Architecture

### Engine 1: Secular TAM & Structural Demand Expansion (Max 25 Pts)
* **Secular Mega-Trend (10 Pts):** Company operates at the epicenter of an irreversible, multi-year technological or infrastructural secular trend (e.g. Generative AI compute, semiconductor packaging bottlenecks, space launch / constellation, or clean 24/7 power).
* **Revenue Acceleration (10 Pts):**
  * $\ge 30\%$ YoY revenue growth = **10 pts**
  * $15\% - 29\%$ YoY revenue growth = **6 pts**
  * $< 15\%$ YoY revenue growth = **0 pts**
* **Contracted Revenue Backlog (5 Pts):** Signed multi-year commercial commitments, customer prepayments, or non-cancellable backlog (e.g. Nebius $9B contracts, TSMC/Nvidia packaging demand).

### Engine 2: Operating Leverage & Cash Conversion (Max 25 Pts)
* **True Operating Leverage (10 Pts):** Earnings per share (EPS) and operating income must expand faster than revenue ($\text{EPS Growth} > \text{Revenue Growth}$) as fixed costs scale.
* **Positive Free Cash Flow (10 Pts):** Cash Flow from Operations (CFO) exceeds Capital Expenditures (CapEx). FCF conversion ratio ($FCF / \text{Net Income}$) $\ge 70\%$.
* **Gross Margin Pricing Power (5 Pts):**
  * Gross Margin $\ge 50\%$ = **5 pts**
  * Gross Margin $< 30\%$ = **0 pts** (Commoditized product, no pricing power).

### Engine 3: Balance Sheet Fortress & Anti-Dilution (Max 20 Pts)
* **Anti-Dilution Discipline (10 Pts):**
  * Annual Share Count Growth $< 2\%$ or Net Share Repurchases = **10 pts**
  * Share Count Growth $2\% - 5\%$ = **5 pts**
  * **Hard Veto:** Annual dilution $\ge 5\%$ = **AUTOMATIC 0 PTS & ALLOCATION VETO.**
* **Net Cash Surplus & Solvency (10 Pts):** Total Liquid Cash & Equivalents exceeds Total Debt (or cash runway $\ge 4.0$ years with positive cash trajectory).

### Engine 4: Valuation & PEG Discipline (Max 15 Pts)
* **Forward PEG Ratio (Price / Earnings / Growth Rate):**
  * Forward $\text{PEG} \le 1.0$ = **15 pts** (Hyper-efficient growth at value price)
  * Forward $\text{PEG} 1.01 - 1.50$ = **10 pts** (Fair institutional entry)
  * Forward $\text{PEG} 1.51 - 2.20$ = **5 pts**
  * Forward $\text{PEG} > 2.20$ = **0 pts** (Over-extended hype multiple)

### Engine 5: Algorithmic Technical Staging & Morning Dip Window (Max 15 Pts)
* **Support Floor Alignment (10 Pts):** Current execution price is situated at or within 2% of verified $S_1$ or $S_2$ Support Floors or 20MFA / 50MFA trendlines.
* **Golden Morning Dip Execution (5 Pts):** Orders staged as Limit Orders executed strictly within the **9:45 AM – 10:30 AM EDT** window, preventing opening-bell retail traps.

A stock is immediately disqualified ($0.00 capital allocation) if ANY of the following occur:
1. **The Dilution Trap:** Diluting outstanding shares by $\ge 5\%$ annually.
2. **The Cash Burn Trap:** Negative Free Cash Flow with less than 2.0 years of cash runway remaining.
3. **The Macro Interest Rate Trap:** High debt burden exposed to refinancing spikes when bond yields are elevated.
