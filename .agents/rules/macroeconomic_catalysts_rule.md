# Rule: Macroeconomic Catalysts Classification & Audit Standard

Every stock market audit, daily market analysis, and portfolio response MUST formally audit and categorize **Macroeconomic Catalysts** in accordance with workspace standards.

---

### 1. Definition of Macroeconomic Catalysts
Macroeconomic Catalysts ("Macro News") are broad, system-wide economic indicators, central bank policies, and geopolitical events that impact interest rates, bond yields, currencies, and overall stock market valuations simultaneously across sectors.

#### Macroeconomic Catalysts Include:
1. 📊 **Inflation Data Releases:** Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE Inflation), Consumer Price Index (CPI), Producer Price Index (PPI).
2. 🏦 **Federal Reserve Monetary Policy Events:** Fed Chair Speeches (e.g., Jackson Hole Keynote Speech by Fed Chair Kevin Warsh), FOMC Rate Decisions, Federal Reserve Rate Cut/Hike Cycles.
3. 📈 **Macro Growth & Labor Data:** Gross Domestic Product (GDP), Non-Farm Payrolls (Jobs Report), Unemployment Rate, Weekly Jobless Claims.
4. 🌐 **Geopolitical & Trade Policy:** Bilateral Trade Tariffs (e.g., Canada US$20B Tariffs), Energy / SWIFT Sanctions, Maritime Bottlenecks (Strait of Hormuz).

---

### 2. Distinction Between Macro vs. Micro Catalysts

| Feature | Macroeconomic Catalyst (Macro News) | Microeconomic Catalyst (Micro News) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scope** | Broad-market (All sectors, indices, bonds) | Stock-specific (Individual company) |
| **Primary Drivers** | PCE Inflation, Fed Rate Cuts, Fed Chair Speeches | Nvidia Earnings, Drug Approval, Contract Award |
| **Yield Impact** | Direct impact on 10-Yr Treasury Yields & DXY | Company revenue, net income, and EPS growth |
| **Trading Effect** | Short-term intraday volatility & sector rotation | Long-term fundamental breakout or drop |

---

### 3. Mandatory Pre-Investment Screening Mandate
- **ALWAYS CHECK MACRO CATALYSTS FIRST:** Before recommending or investing in ANY stock, the assistant MUST check upcoming Macroeconomic Catalysts (PCE Inflation, CPI, Fed Chair Speeches, Interest Rate decisions).
- **PRE-EVENT DROP PRINCIPLE:** Stocks frequently drop or consolidate 24–48 hours prior to major macro releases due to institutional de-risking and options market delta hedging.
- **LIMIT ORDER DIRECTIVE:** Never chase stocks immediately before high-impact macro releases. Use pre-macro pullbacks to place Limit Buy Orders at S1/S2/S3 candle support floors.
- **TABLE INTEGRATION:** In every analysis, Macroeconomic Catalysts MUST be tracked in **Table 1 (Official Breaking Market & Geopolitical Headlines Table)** and **Table 2 (Official Weekly Market-Moving Catalysts Table)**.
- Analysts MUST clarify whether a macro data release (e.g., PCE Inflation at 3.7% YoY vs Core 3.3%) poses temporary short-term yield volatility or structural fundamental disruption.
- High-conviction 4/4 Gate Passed Gold Monopolies (`NVDA`, `GOOGL`, `MP`, `RKLB`, `SOFI`) MUST NOT be panic-sold during temporary macro volatility.

---

### 4. Mandatory Exact Calendar Date & Pre-Event Buy Date Mapping Rule
Every Macroeconomic Catalyst analysis MUST explicitly state TWO exact dates:
1. 📅 **Official Macro Release Date & Time:** The exact calendar day and time the macro news drops (e.g., *Friday, August 28, 2026 @ 10:00 AM EDT — Fed Chair Kevin Warsh Keynote Speech*).
2. 🎯 **Optimal Pre-Event Buying Window (Buy Date):** The exact 24–48 hour date window BEFORE the macro news release when stocks drop/consolidate and Limit Orders should be placed (e.g., *Wednesday, August 26 – Thursday, August 27, 2026 — Pre-Event Limit Buy Window*).
