# 📐 RED-TAG MANDATORY RULE: DUAL-SCENARIO TECHNICAL INFLECTION MATRIX STANDARD

## 🚨 MANDATORY EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE
Whenever the assistant evaluates any stock, index (e.g. S&P 500 `ES1!`, `SPY`, `QQQ`), or potential trade entry, the assistant is **STRICTLY REQUIRED** to perform and present a **Dual-Scenario Technical Inflection Matrix**.

Never evaluate a stock or index based solely on single-line price targets or vague trend descriptions. Every technical evaluation MUST explicitly map out:
1. **The Overhead Resistance Ceilings & Macro Targets**
2. **The Immediate Inflection Pivot ("The Make-or-Break Line")**
3. **The Fibonacci Retracement Levels & Structural Support Floors (0.50 & 0.65 Golden Pocket)**
4. **The Dual-Pathway "If/Then" Decision Trees (Bullish White-Arrow Path vs Bearish Breakdown Path)**

**NO EXCEPTIONS PERMITTED.**

## 🏛️ 1. The 4 Structural Technical Pillars

Every technical evaluation MUST identify and define these four pillars:

### 1️⃣ Overhead Resistance Ceilings (R1 / R2 / All-Time Highs)
* The macro high or double-top where previous rallies were rejected (e.g., AMD at $658.52, `ES1!` at 7,884–7,897).
* The long-term upside milestone target (e.g., `ES1!` Target 8000).

### 2️⃣ The Immediate Inflection Pivot ("The Make-or-Break Line")
* The immediate structural line in the sand separating short-term bullish momentum from bearish continuation (e.g., `ES1!` at **7,760**, AMD at **$554–$564**).
* **The Rule:** If price is below this pivot, it acts as an active resistance ceiling. Buying directly underneath an unconfirmed pivot is strictly prohibited.

### 3️⃣ Fibonacci Retracements & Structural Support Floors
* **0.50 Retracement (Halfway Mark):** First consolidation pause (often marks gap-fill zones).
* **0.618 – 0.65 Retracement (The Golden Pocket):** The highest-conviction institutional accumulation zone (e.g., AMD at **$517.48**).
* **Structural Support Floors (S1 / S2 / S3):** Horizontal price shelves established by previous swing highs or multi-day consolidation.
* **The Macro Base:** The absolute baseline where the entire multi-month rally originated (e.g., AMD at $449, `ES1!` at 7,544).

### 4️⃣ The Dual-Pathway "If/Then" Scenarios
Every analysis MUST explicitly describe both sides of the trade:
* 🟢 **Scenario A: Bullish Confirmation Pathway (The White Arrow):**
  * Step 1: Support or Golden Pocket holds with reversal volume.
  * Step 2: Price breaks above the **Immediate Inflection Pivot**.
  * Step 3: Price forms a **higher-low pullback retest** of the broken pivot (old resistance turning into new support).
  * Step 4: Expansion push toward overhead resistance and macro targets.
* 🔴 **Scenario B: Bearish Rejection Pathway (The Breakdown Path):**
  * Step 1: Rejection at the Immediate Inflection Pivot OR failure of the immediate support floor.
  * Step 2: Loss of the 0.65 Fibonacci Golden Pocket.
  * Step 3: Cascading algorithmic liquidation down into secondary (S2) and tertiary (S3 / Macro Base) support floors.
