## ✅ The 3 Mandatory Kid Questions (Answer Every Time in 3 Short Bullets!)
Every **`🧸 Simple Kid-Level Summary`** MUST answer these 3 questions clearly in 1 line each:
1. **🌦️ What is the weather right now?** *(Sunny playground, storm clouds, or full thunderstorm?)*
2. **🔍 Why is my stock moving?** *(Is the company actually broken, or is it just raining on everyone?)*
3. **🚦 What is my exact simple move?** *(Keep holding good toys, keep cash in the Piggy Bank, wait for Report Card day, or harvest profits on a sunny bounce?)*
