# Rule: Dual-Moat Quant Algorithmic Candlestick & Pure Cash Solvency Rule

This rule governs all stock analyses, trade recommendations, and portfolio audits executed for the user.

## Core Directives

### 1. Wall Street HFT Algorithmic & Index ETF Candlestick Pattern Alignment
- **TradingView OHLC & Pivot Levels:** All stock charts must follow real-time TradingView 1D Candlestick OHLC (Open, High, Low, Close) and Pivot Point levels (Pivot P, S1 Support Floor, S2 Technical Stop Floor, R1 Tier 1 Resistance Exit, R2 Tier 2 Resistance Exit).

- **ETF Basket Flow Correlation:** Track passive index ETF basket flows (SPY, QQQ, BOTZ) that drive identical V-shaped candlestick pattern bounces across portfolio holdings.

### 2. Pure Cash Solvency & Fundamental Protection
- **Pure Bank Cash Protection:** Every stock audited or recommended must have heavy pure liquid bank cash backing per share (or high annual GAAP Net Cash Income) to eliminate bankruptcy or debt default risks.
- **Zero / Ultra-Low Debt Standard:** Prioritize companies with $0 corporate debt or ultra-low debt relative to cash reserves and annual cash flows.
- **Contract & Moat Backing:** Verify multi-billion dollar contract backlogs (DoD, Amazon AWS, Eli Lilly, Google Cloud, Starlink) or proven monopoly moats before placing limit buy orders.

### 3. Asymmetric Short-Term Swing Execution
- **Strict Asymmetric Risk/Reward:** Maintain a minimum 3:1 to 30:1 Asymmetric Risk/Reward Ratio (upside potential meaningfully larger than planned loss).
- **Limit Order Floor Execution:** Never chase day-1/day-2 gap-up spikes. Always set Limit Buy Orders at predefined 50 SMA / 100 SMA support floors (S1/S2).
