## 2. ⚖️ `Narrow Breadth / Concentration` Warning
* **What It Is:** Only 5–10 mega-cap giants hold `SPY`/`QQQ` near highs while `IWM` (small caps) and 400+ equal-weight stocks quietly fall below their 50-SMAs *(classic 1970s "Nifty Fifty" & late-cycle top pattern)*.
* **Rule:** Whenever `SPY` is within `2%` of highs while `IWM` lags by `≥ 5%` over 30 days, **stop chasing extended mega-caps** and raise the Barbell Safe Shield by `+10%`.

## 3. 🪤 Event & Crowd Traps (`Buy the Rumor / Sell the News` & `Short Squeeze`)
1. **`Buy the Rumor, Sell the News`:**
   * When a stock rallies **`+15%+`** in the 2 weeks leading *into* a known catalyst (earnings, product keynote, FDA/NRC decision, Fed cut), institutions routinely sell into retail excitement once the good news prints.
   * **Rule:** Harvest `25%–33%` into pre-event euphoria at Neckline resistance; **NEVER chase the opening spike** on already-expected good news.
2. **`Short Squeeze`:**
   * When a heavily shorted stock (`> 15%–20%` short interest) explodes vertically (`+15%` to `+40%` in 1–2 days) without matching fundamental earnings power, short-sellers are being mechanically forced to cover.
   * **Rule:** Treat every vertical short squeeze as an **EXIT / PROFIT-HARVEST ZONE (sell 33%–50%)**, **NEVER** an initial buy zone. Squeezes collapse back to their base once forced covering ends.
