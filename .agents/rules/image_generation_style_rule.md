# Image Generation Style Rule (Natural & Funny Webcomic Aesthetic)

## Purpose
This rule governs all image generation tasks for stock market updates, breaking news infographics, and social media posts within this project.

---

## Mandated Image Generation Guidelines

1. **Aesthetic & Art Style**:
   - **Style**: 2D hand-drawn digital webcomic / editorial cartoon illustration.
   - **Texture**: Warm watercolor and ink outlines, natural paper texture, organic hand-crafted feel.
   - **Strictly Prohibited**: Glossy 3D renderings, dark neon glassmorphism AI UI glows, robotic stock photo aesthetics, or hyper-futuristic artificial designs (unless explicitly requested).

2. **Tone & Composition**:
   - **Humor & Relatability**: Playful, witty, and relatable to daily traders and market enthusiasts.
   - **Characters**: Include expressive 2D cartoon figures (e.g., Wall Street Bull and Bear, coffee-drinking trader, humorous characters).
   - **Text & Headlines**: Render market headlines, numbers, and stock tickers (`NVDA`, `MRVL`, etc.) as clear hand-written chalkboard lettering, newspaper headlines, or organic poster text.

3. **Aspect Ratios**:
   - Default to `1:1` (Square for Instagram Feed/LinkedIn) or `9:16` (Vertical for Instagram Stories/Reels) depending on the post platform.
