## 🏛️ 2. The 4 Mandatory Laws for Escalating Scandal & Allegation Spirals

### Law 1: The "Cockroach / Escalating Allegation" Price Reality
* Once a company or CEO enters an active **Board, FBI/DOJ/SEC, Congressional, or Executive-Misconduct crisis**, investigative leaks rarely stop at one story ("never just one cockroach").
* **Unconfirmed Secondary Leaks Still Crash the Stock:** Wall Street algorithms and institutional risk desks **do NOT wait for CEO confirmation** to reprice key-person and governance risk. Each additional allegation — especially a **direct-report HR/fiduciary conflict** or **"no comment" / hanging up on reporters** — triggers a fresh **`-5%` to `-10%+`** leg lower immediately upon publication.
* **Strict Rule:** Never discount a worsening media leak simply because the CEO or company has not confirmed it. Treat unconfirmed secondary allegations + `"declined to comment"` during an active probe as **Active Leg-Down Fuel**.

### Law 2: Immediate Purge of Resting `all_day_hours` / GTC Buy Orders
* The instant any stock enters an active governance, national security, accounting, or executive-ouster probe:
  1. **Immediately audit and recommend cancelling ALL resting `all_day_hours` and GTC limit buy orders** on that ticker (`get_equity_orders`).
  2. Never leave passive dip bids sitting on a live scandal tape where `5:00 PM EST` news bombs or after-hours leaks run them over in illiquid extended hours.

### Law 3: Mandatory `$0.00` Freeze & Competitor Divergence Check
* Enact an immediate **`$0.00` Capital Allocation Freeze** across the scandal-hit ticker for a minimum of **3 full trading days** after *each* new escalating allegation or Board crisis report (resetting the 3-day clock whenever a fresh bombshell hits).
* Cross-check direct peers (`AMD`, `AVGO`): when rivals rally `+2%` to `+4%` while the target plunges `-8%`, recognize **pure company-specific key-person / replacement risk** — never treat it as a harmless macro dip.

### Law 4: The 3-Condition De-Escalation Clearance Test
Capital stays **`$0.00` Frozen** until ALL three clear:
1. **Zero New Leaks for 72 Hours:** Three consecutive trading days without a fresh investigation, executive, or subpoena escalation.
2. **Formal Board Resolution:** Official Board statement settling CEO continuity, succession, or governance findings (eliminating `"Board weighing ouster"` uncertainty).
3. **Regular-Session Support Stabilization:** A clean floor forms during regular hours (`9:45–10:30 AM EST`) with shrinking sell volume while keeping single-stock weight strictly **<= `15.0%`** of total equity.
