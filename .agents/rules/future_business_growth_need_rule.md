# FUTURE BUSINESS GROWTH NEED & EXPANSION MANDATE RULE

## Executive Mandate
Whenever analyzing any stock, performing an audit, or recommending capital allocation, the assistant MUST include a dedicated **Future Business Growth Need & Industry Demand Breakdown**. 

This section explicitly answers **WHY the company's business MUST grow over the next 3 to 5 years** based on structural industry demand, indispensable technology, and expanding market opportunities.

## Required Future Business Growth Need Breakdown Structure

The assistant MUST present a dedicated section titled:
`### 🔮 Future Business Growth Need & 3-5 Year Industry Expansion Analysis`

### 1. Mandatory Future Business Growth Need Table

| Growth Catalyst # | Macro & Industry Structural Demand | Why Customer Demand MUST Scale (3-5 Yr Horizon) | TAM (Total Addressable Market) & Revenue Growth | Irreplaceable Moat & Future Expansion Runway |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🚀 **Driver 1** | Primary industry mega-trend | Exact reason enterprise/consumer buyers MUST adopt product | TAM projections & ARR growth rates | Technical patent/moat protecting future revenue |
| ⚡ **Driver 2** | Secondary technology/geopolitical shift | Customer lock-in & switching cost friction | Projected revenue expansion trajectory | Infrastructure scale & capital efficiency |
| 🏆 **Driver 3** | Monopoly or dominant niche position | Irreplaceability in the global supply chain | Forward revenue targets & margin expansion | Regulatory or enterprise barrier to entry |

### 2. Core Strategic Growth Questions Answered
Every audit MUST address these 4 core growth pillars:
1. 🎯 **Irreplaceability:** Why cannot this company be replaced by competitors in the next 3–5 years?
2. 🌊 **Macro Wave:** What multi-billion dollar secular shift (AI, Defense, Energy, Automation, Rare Earths) is propelling its top-line growth?
3. 📜 **Prepaid Customer Commitment:** How do signed contracts, prepayments, or backlog guarantee future revenue visibility?
4. 📈 **Operating Leverage:** How fast will net profit grow relative to revenue growth as capital expenditures decline?
