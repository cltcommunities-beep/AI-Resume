## 📈 20MFA / 50MFA Technical Entry Definitions

- **20MFA (20-day Moving Average Floor Alignment):** High-momentum pullback entry. Stock is testing or bouncing off its short-term 20-day exponential/simple moving average support line.
- **50MFA (50-day Moving Average Floor Alignment):** Institutional value dip entry. Stock has pulled back to its key 50-day moving average accumulation floor—offering a low-risk, high-reward entry point.
