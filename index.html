NVDA AI Compute Silicon
🧠 The AI Brain Designer

🏢 What It Does:

NVIDIA designs the world's most powerful specialized computer chips (GPUs) that act as the 'super-brains' behind modern artificial intelligence like ChatGPT, self-driving cars, and robotics.

🎯 Why Needed:

Standard computer processors (CPUs) can only do one calculation at a time. NVIDIA chips can perform millions of complex mathematical calculations at the exact same fraction of a second, which is the only way AI models can learn and generate answers.
